# Patch 2026-09-30: season 5's preseason — [IMPLEMENTED] 2026-10-01

How the 2026-09-30 patch was captured, what the wire showed, and what the program changed for it. What is still open went to `tasks.md`, I35.

Sources: the update notice (`page.onstove.com/chaoszeronightmare/en/view/14555091`) and the Sereniel & Peko developer note (`…/view/14536071`). Both mix bonuses into stats and have been wrong before, so every number was read off the wire or the game's own screens before it went into the program — and the notice's were right where checked: Anika's base, the season's shape, the Offensive's and the pass's 42 days.

## Done ahead of the patch

The Galactic Disaster rows had never been through a season change, and replaying the newest snapshot past the maintenance showed three faults, each fixed and pinned by a check before the patch:

- After the first weekly reset, `Galactic Disaster - Chaos` and `Seasonal Accumulated Score` went red for work that does not exist until part 1. They read `Not open` between seasons and through a preseason (`checklist_tab.disaster_off_season`).
- With season 5's window open and one Sortie rotation listed (the wire lists a rotation only once it has begun), no shop page was dated, and the ended season's shelves came back as buyable. The shop stays shut through the preseason, and the column counts down to part 1.
- The live season came from the Great Rift standings, which name the previous season until the new season's Great Rift is played. The Checklist and the Chaos run recorder take it from the season schedule first; season 5's part 1 runs would otherwise have been filed as season 4's.

## How it was captured

The method, for the next patch:

1. **The first login, alone**, `Debug WS` on before launching: some things happen once, at it (the Sortie's Tactical Optimization reset and refund, Growth Guide missions, season 4's currencies wiped, its medals moving to the Display Case), and it is the untouched state every later capture is diffed against.
2. **Everything, one action per screen**, in a separate capture, noting what the screen shows that the wire cannot: each new banner's Probability Info and Rescue Records to the last page; each new event opened and one reward claimed; one Simulation run; the Zero System and the Chaos Matrix; the Galactic Disaster; the pass; the Full-Scale Offensive; the Basin; the Sortie's Tactical Optimization and Hardcore Rankings; each new Missions tab; the Growth Guide; a plain battle with each combatant whose node 7 or base changed.
3. **A login capture every day after 18:00 UTC**: what a table gains overnight says whether it was issued whole (`docs/events.md`, *Processing a new event*).

## What the captures settled

- **The season kept its shape**: `disaster_s05` 09-30 to 12-23, season 4's tail to 10-21, one Sortie rotation in the preseason. The fixes above read it right.
- **Sereniel's node 7 is the note's**: her first post-patch sheet, at 60.4% CRate, carried Instinct damage 31 — 16 from fragments and 15 from node 7. Her sheets from before the patch are held to the old node 7 (`POTENTIAL_7_BEFORE`).
- **Her Seasonal Rescue Rate-Up is `gacha_pickup_combatant_30075_1`**, rerun suffix and all, and its Rescue Records answered with the release banners', whose pity it shares. A Seasonal rerun is told apart by the rates: its unit is in no Normal Rescue's list (`gacha_history.note_general_pool`).
- **Anika is res_id 1012**, a 4-star Order Striker, nodes 5 and 6 CRate and CDmg, node 7 +10% ATK at 500 ATK. Her level-60 sheet read 405/163/381, the notice's figures less Affinity 40's, and her node-7 sheet matched the program's formula at 646 inner ATK. A level-up states no stats, only the row's `exp`.
- **The new events**: Guardian Angel's Vacation is `event_nodelist_008` (three pages of four rewards and one for all three, 13 in all), Sereniel's Memoirs `event_schedule_director_002` (seven days of three); both issue a task's row as it is reached. Chapter 0 is a main-story node, `event_disaster_s05_c0` its deadline only. The Nightmare Carnival countdown is `countdown_attendance_1st`, a new group `LOBBY_COUNTDOWN`, 10-06 to 10-21.
- **The Overclock counts multiplier units**: one x5 run used five of the day's six. A day's runs are its own row's; the second day opened on `0/2` until the row showed the first day's six.
- **The Full-Scale Offensive** starts with an empty board, a boss's row appearing once fought. `reward_count` counts the star rewards claimed, and `reset_remnants` zeroes a boss's stars while the claimed rewards stay. The 9-star count crossed the wire nowhere.
- **Arkhianon Supply 009 had no record** until its first claim; the Chaos Matrix claim (`zero_orb/get_orb_reward`) answers with `zero_orb_entity`.
- **A pull pays on its result list** (`reward_items`), and **a Chaos escape answers `CLEAR`**, with `is_emergency_exit` on the request only. The regular Chaos is `chaos/enter_embody_chaos_stage`.
- **A combatant's row arrives alone** — `character` from a level-up, promotion or reward claim, `char` from a Potential node or a gift — and a node's cost under `currencies`.
- **A x3 Simulation run with one Overclock run left** pays three runs in its drops, and its clear adds the doubled run under `result_reward_drop_overclock`, at the same total as the restated drops.

## Decisions

- **2026-09-30 — The Vacation's 13 is written down** (`WRITTEN_TOTALS`): no table censuses it, and its Node List past reads `~1/25`. Whether to keep the write-down is the maintainer's call, in I35.
- **2026-10-01 — An Overclock day's shape** comes from the live event's own earlier days where they average above two (the six is then proved), else from the last event played.
- **2026-10-01 — The Offensive's stars** are the board's or the rewards claimed, whichever is more.
- **2026-10-01 — Pulls are kept off their replies** until their records come, and a Rescue Records read that reaches nothing already kept flags its banner orange. A gap whose newer end is older than the game keeps is dropped, since no read could fill it. A banner pulled on while capturing is watched by the counters even if never opened.
- **2026-10-01 — A Simulation run is one log line**, said at its clear: the drops are held back, and the clear's envelopes' `diff`s are the run.
- **2026-10-01 — No shipped Have-at-least minimum for a combatant whose Potential 7 the score prices in full**: the score pays for meeting it, so a minimum only hides the builds that miss it. `check_shipped_defaults` holds it against the release copy.
- **2026-10-01 — The Fill button sits at Have at least's foot**, and the panel takes three columns (ATK/DEF/HP, Crit%/CDMG%, Extra%/DoT%/Ego), which keeps it under Important Settings' height.
- **2026-10-01 — Season 4's division tops** from cznmetadecks.com (`docs/rift_tops_fetch.py`): the site's last reading of part 2 was 09-29 23:3x UTC, about half an hour before the close, so it is recorded as a dated reading, not a final. Part 1's matched what was already recorded.
